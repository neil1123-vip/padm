About the journal

A little more time with a photograph

__SITE_TITLE__ is a collection of landscape studies. Each entry starts with a photograph and looks at one or two choices that make it readable: a boundary, a shift in brightness, or the space around the subject.

The notes are about looking. They do not depend on a camera model or a particular editing application. The same questions can be asked while making a photograph or while spending time with somebody else's work.

The images

The photographs in this collection are from Unsplash and are used under the Unsplash License. The essays are visual observations of those photographs; they are not accounts of visits to the locations.
