About this collection

A place to return to

__SITE_TITLE__ collects short essays about reading, walking and the habits that help ordinary experiences stay in memory. The subjects are small by design. A useful detail is often easier to find when the scale is manageable.

The notes are arranged by date. They can be read in any order; the links at the end of each article suggest another place to go.

In the notebook

  • Keeping a small index — a way back to older entries.
  • Rereading in place — comparing two encounters with a passage.
  • Listening for the weather — ordinary sounds as small clues.
  • A note without a conclusion — keeping uncertainty visible.
  • Maps of familiar streets — landmarks and gaps in memory.
  • Making room for questions — space for a thought still taking shape.
  • Walking without a route — attention outside.
  • A reading notebook that stays useful — attention on the page.
